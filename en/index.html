Smart call blocking

WATCHTOWER, private call blocking for Android

WATCHTOWER is an Android app that filters and blocks unwanted calls directly on your phone, with no account, no ads, and no internet connection required for filtering.

Read the privacy policy

No advertising

No advertising tracking tool is integrated into the app.

No collection

WATCHTOWER does not sell, share, or store personal data.

Local processing

Core features are designed to run on your device when the system allows it.

ACTIVE PROTECTION

Enjoy fewer interruptions without missing important calls

Once enabled, WATCHTOWER quietly screens your calls and rejects unwanted ones before your phone even rings. Official French telemarketing prefixes regulated by ARCEP, including 01 62, 01 63, 02 70, and 02 71, are already covered, and you stay in control with your own rules for numbers, prefixes, countries, or geographic regions.

Allowed numbers always take priority. With no ads, no account, no remote server, and no internet connection required for filtering, protection runs directly on your phone and your data never leaves it.
