Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

WATCHTOWER is designed to help block unwanted calls while respecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed, how it is used, and what choices remain under your control.

1. No personal data collection

WATCHTOWER does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app does not create a user profile and does not send personal information to an external server.

2. Local and offline operation

WATCHTOWER's core features are designed to work locally on your device. The processing required for call blocking remains on the device when permitted by the operating system.

3. Call data

WATCHTOWER may use call blocking features provided by your device to identify or block unwanted numbers. This information is not sent by WATCHTOWER to third parties.

4. Advertising and tracking

The app does not contain advertising and does not use advertising tracking tools. No advertising identifier is used to track you.

5. Permissions

Depending on your device, WATCHTOWER may request permissions required for call blocking or caller identification. These permissions are used only for the app features described to you.

6. Third-party services

WATCHTOWER does not rely on third-party services to collect personal data. If a distribution platform provides its own analytics or diagnostics, those processes are governed by that platform's own terms and privacy settings.

7. Security

Because WATCHTOWER does not collect personal data, risks related to storing personal data are reduced. We still recommend keeping your device and the app up to date.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect functional, technical, or legal changes. The last updated date indicates the most recent version.

9. Contact
